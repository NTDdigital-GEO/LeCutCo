Before you book

Questions homeowners ask us

Is the design consult really free? Do I have to commit?

Yes, it's free and there's no obligation. We visit your home, measure and talk through layouts, then send you a written summary and a preliminary estimate. You keep both even if you don't hire us.

How much will my kitchen cost?

It depends on size, layout and finishes, so we don't quote blind. After the visit you get a preliminary estimate. Once you approve your 3D design, you get one itemized, fixed price.

How long will it take?

Most kitchen and bathroom remodels take 4–8 weeks of construction once permits are approved. Additions and whole-home remodels typically take 3–6 months. Your project lead walks you through the schedule before work starts.

Can I build an ADU or convert my garage?

California law allows an ADU on most single-family lots, but setbacks, utilities and your lot's layout decide what fits. We check this at the free consult, before you spend anything on design.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and Title 24 energy calculations (the state's efficiency paperwork), submit them to the city and are there for the inspections.

Which areas do you serve?

San Jose, including Willow Glen, Almaden Valley, Cambrian, Rose Garden and Berryessa, plus Santa Clara, Sunnyvale, Cupertino, Campbell, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Milpitas, Mountain View, Palo Alto and nearby South Bay cities.
